# Massera

**A marketplace for massage, spa, and wellness bookings**

Massera connects people looking for wellness services with providers and spa teams. The product is designed around the real-world booking journey, from discovering a service to coordinating the appointment.

## Product experiences

- **Clients** can explore services and providers, review service details, and manage booking-related activity.
- **Providers** can maintain their service presence and handle work related to their appointments.
- **Spa teams** can coordinate services and day-to-day operations through a dedicated workspace.
- **Administrators** can support marketplace operations through web-based tools.

The initial market focus is Nigeria, with the product structure intended to support additional country and city configuration over time.

## Engineering scope

The platform combines a Flutter mobile application, a Next.js administration experience, and Supabase-backed data and server workflows. The architecture separates client, provider, spa, and administrative responsibilities.

## Technology

Flutter and Dart, Next.js, Supabase, and PostgreSQL.
